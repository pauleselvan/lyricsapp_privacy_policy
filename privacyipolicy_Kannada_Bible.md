# Privacy Policy — Kannada-English Holy Bible

**Effective date:** October 10, 2026

Kannada-English Holy Bible ("the App") is developed by Tamil Christian Repository. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our application.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App provides Bible reading and study features. You can use its core Bible reading features without creating an account.

### Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, the App may store the following information locally on your device:

- Bookmarks and saved Bible verses.
- Personal notes associated with Bible verses.
- Highlighted verses and selected highlight colors.
- Reading history and Continue Reading progress.
- App settings and notification preferences.

This information supports the App's functionality. It is not automatically transmitted to our servers by these local-storage features unless a specific feature or integration provides otherwise.

### Advertising and device information

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

- Device identifiers and advertising IDs.
- IP address and general device or network information.
- Advertising interactions, impressions, and diagnostic information.
- Information used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalization where permitted.

The information collected depends on your device settings, consent choices, applicable law, and the advertising services enabled in the App.

Please review [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information about Google's data practices.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App and its service providers may be used to:

- Provide Bible reading, search, bookmarks, notes, highlights, and reading progress.
- Save your preferences and notification settings.
- Schedule Daily Bible notifications and other notifications when enabled.
- Display and measure advertisements.
- Diagnose errors, maintain app stability, and prevent fraud or abuse.
- Improve the App and its features.

We do not use your personal Bible notes or bookmarks for advertising purposes unless a future feature or service specifically requires such processing and the applicable privacy disclosures are updated.

## 3. Notifications

The App may provide Daily Bible notifications and other notifications. You can manage these notifications through the App's settings and your device's notification settings.

Notification preferences may be stored locally. Disabling notifications prevents the corresponding notifications from being scheduled or delivered by the App, subject to the behavior of any notifications already delivered or controlled by the operating system.

## 4. Sharing and Copying Content

The App may allow you to copy Bible verses, share scripture, or create and share verse posters.

When you choose to share content, the App may open another application or service, such as a messaging app or social network. Information you choose to share is handled according to the privacy practices of that service.

Please review the privacy policy of the service you use to share content.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when required to provide app functionality, including:

- **Google AdMob:** To display and measure advertisements and support advertising-related services.
- **Operating system and platform services:** To support notifications and other device-level functionality.
- **Apps you choose to use for sharing:** To transmit content when you explicitly initiate a sharing action.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

Bible content may be available offline. Notes, bookmarks, highlights, reading history, and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

We take reasonable steps within our control to maintain the App's security. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

If you uninstall the App or clear its data, locally stored information may be deleted. Information handled by third-party services is subject to their respective retention practices.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App may be used by people of different ages. We do not knowingly request children's personal information through an account-registration system.

Advertising services may process device or advertising information according to their configuration and applicable requirements. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of devices and review relevant advertising and privacy settings.

If you believe that personal information has been collected inappropriately from a child, please contact us so that we can investigate.

## 8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

You can also:

- Manage notification preferences in the App.
- Manage advertising preferences and advertising ID settings through your device, where available.
- Limit or reset advertising identifiers where supported.
- Remove locally stored information by deleting individual records, clearing app data, or uninstalling the App, subject to the feature's available controls.

For requests concerning information handled by third-party providers, you may need to contact those providers directly.

## 9. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may use third-party services or open external links. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites or services. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published at the privacy-policy URL associated with the App, with an updated effective date when appropriate.
